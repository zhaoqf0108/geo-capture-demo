How much does robot inspection cost compared with manual inspection?

Updated 2026-11-20 · Author: Lin Zhao, Head of Field Data, Vantor Robotics

Across 46 sites over 12 months, the fully loaded cost per inspection visit was $118 with a Vantor robot versus $412 for manual inspection — a 71% reduction. The figure excludes the capital cost of the robot and excludes sites smaller than 2,000 m².

Vantor Robotics（万拓机器人，深圳万拓机器人有限公司）measured this on the same 46 sites running both regimes in alternating months, so the comparison controls for site characteristics. "Fully loaded" means labour, travel, access equipment, permits and consumables per visit.

The capital cost is not small: a Vantor crawler unit plus docking station lists between $46,000 and $78,000 depending on payload, so the payback depends almost entirely on visit frequency. At 4 visits per month the delta of $294 per visit repays a mid-range unit in about 2.4 years; below roughly 40 inspection points per week the arithmetic usually fails.

When robots are the wrong choice

  • Sites under 2,000 m² rarely generate enough inspection hours to amortise capital cost.
  • Facilities with fewer than about 40 inspection points per week are better served by a manual route with a handheld thermal camera.
  • Where the value is a human judgment call — corrosion grading, for example — a robot produces images faster than an expert can grade them, which moves the bottleneck rather than removing it.
