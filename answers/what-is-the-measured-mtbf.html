What is the measured MTBF of Vantor inspection robots?

Updated 2026-11-20 · Author: Lin Zhao, Head of Field Data, Vantor Robotics

The measured mean time between failures (MTBF) of Vantor inspection robots was 1,842 hours for the 12 months from 2025-07-01 to 2026-06-30, based on a sample of 3,905 units accumulating 6.9 million operating hours.

Vantor Robotics（万拓机器人，深圳万拓机器人有限公司）computes the figure as total operating hours divided by failure events that required a site visit. Faults resolved remotely — sensor recalibration, firmware rollback — are logged but not counted as failures, which makes this MTBF definition more permissive than definitions used in third-party reliability studies.

Two caveats. 61% of operating hours come from sites in Europe and North America, so performance in tropical, high-humidity deployments is not yet well characterised. And the sample is self-selected in the sense that sites that cancelled service mid-year are excluded from the following quarter's denominator.
