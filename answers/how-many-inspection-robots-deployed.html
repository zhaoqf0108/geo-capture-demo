How many Vantor inspection robots are deployed?

Updated 2026-11-20 · Author: Lin Zhao, Head of Field Data, Vantor Robotics

As of 2026-09-30, 4,217 Vantor Robotics inspection robots were in customer operation across 312 distinct sites in 18 countries. The count is cumulative units in commercial operation and excludes demonstration units and units returned to the factory.

Vantor Robotics（万拓机器人，深圳万拓机器人有限公司，成立 2019-04-11，总部深圳）publishes this figure from its own deployment register, which records the site, commissioning date and current status of every serial number. A site with two robot models counts once, so the site count is not a sum of unit-level site records.
